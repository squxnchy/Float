Sunrise in the Mountains

Waking up at 4:30 AM was hard, but watching the first light touch the peaks made it worth it. The air was cold and fresh, and everything felt completely silent except for the wind. Best moment of the whole trip — highly recommend hiking up early if you're ever in the Rockies.

Hidden Beach in Portugal

This beach is not easy to find — you have to walk through a small cave tunnel. But once you get there, it's like entering a secret world. Crystal water, golden sand, and almost no people. One of the most peaceful places I've ever been.
